# Methodology Changelog

## 0.2.0

This is a breaking documentation-first methodology revision.

### Retired

- Weighted readiness aggregate
- Compensating average risk result
- Automated favorable or adverse dispositions
- Highest stage label suggesting absolute quantum safety

### Established

- Quantum Exposure Severity with a separate `Unknown / Not Assessed` result
- Ten-domain Migration Readiness Profile with no weighted total
- Evidence Confidence and Coverage, including unknown, stale, conflicting, and excluded records
- Twelve non-compensatory Critical Risk Overrides
- Quantum Readiness Decision Pack as the complete public deliverable
- Human-governed, non-authoritative planning postures
- Machine-checked orientation and minimum meanings for readiness Stages 0–5
- Machine-checked positive semantics for CR-01 through CR-12

The v0.2.0 methodology is documentation-first and human-governed. It does not use automated favorable or adverse dispositions.
